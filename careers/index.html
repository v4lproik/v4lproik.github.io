Career

From breaking systems to building protocol infrastructure

My career moved from offensive security into distributed systems, then into protocol, validator, and market infrastructure. For more than seven years I have worked across Ethereum, Solana, Tezos, Polkadot, and Cosmos, usually where software meets operators, capital, and production constraints.

The technology changes; my operating style does not: learn the whole system, find its failure modes, and turn emerging technology into infrastructure that teams can run. I have done that as an individual contributor, principal engineer, team lead, founding engineer, and hands-on CTO.
